## Keep the scope clear

V62 is the 35,761-object whole-ship baseline. V102 is a two-bay comparison with one hull, 13 attached shapes, and one group—not a complete ship assembly. Two of 14 bays are verified comparisons; zero have been adopted into the baseline. No new license is granted.
